Citation Checker

Paste your references and see which ones have been retracted.

  1. Go to My Citation Checks and create a check (one per chapter or paper).
  2. Open it and paste your DOIs, one per line. Links and doi: prefixes are fine.
  3. Click Run Citation Check to see any retracted papers.
